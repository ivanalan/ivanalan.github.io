What changed from v1

Backdrop colors sit close in value to the page (slate, sage, ink) instead of bright blues and greens, and the slider lets you dial how present they are. The screenshot is now the brightest thing on the card.

Placement varies by image type

Laptop mockups peek up from the bottom edge. Flat dark UI gets a soft frame or bleeds off the corner. Phones peek too. One component, a layout prop.

Data model

Frontmatter gets coverBackdrop, coverImage (an Astro image()) and coverLayout. getImage in work-cards.ts already resizes covers to 1280px; the same call works here.

Caveats

Dark screenshots on a dark backdrop depend on the halo ring to separate. Large PNGs (up to 2.8k wide) should become WebP at roughly 1400px before shipping. I haven't verified FlutedGlass exists in your pinned shaders-react@0.0.80.

C · Four ways to place the same screenshot (common portfolio patterns, from my general familiarity, not a sourced survey)
Peek. Device cropped at the bottom edge; implies there's more below. Works when you have a mockup.
Frame. Whole screen visible with a halo. Best for recognition at a glance; the safest option for dark UI.
Bleed. Oversized, cropped off the corner. Feels like a real product; hides detail, so pick a screen that reads when cropped.
Stack. Two screens overlapped to show range (a flow plus its result). More visual noise, so use it on one or two projects, not all.
