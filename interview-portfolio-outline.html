Plot twist · research

“Will users accept being blocked by errors?”

They wouldn’t ship an untakeable survey — but they still hesitated to be blocked from Publish.

  • Publish ≠ final send. Often “save a version in history.”
  • Autosave existed; Publish still carried meaning.
  • Update: platform wasn’t ready to block publish until save/publish decoupled → most issues stayed Warnings.

Talking points

  • Lead with the surprise — don’t bury it under “we ran a study.”
  • Name the research question out loud; say you partnered with a researcher to prioritize questions.
  • Show judgment: evidence changed the framework, not just the UI.

  • Hook → stakes → outcome early → 2–3 decisions → result → one honest reverse
  • One idea per slide; talking points stay in comments / speaker notes
  • Decision slides always show: chose / rejected / tradeoff
  • Prefer concrete scenes (“10 open texts”) over process nouns (“ideation”)
  • Warmth > jokes: make complicated feel simple; surprise beats status reports
  • Protect time for interruption — panels will dive on decisions
  • Target ~12–14 Figma slides for Guidance, ~10–12 for Platform
