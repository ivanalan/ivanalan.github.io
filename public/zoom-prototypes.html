Click-zoom + pan — controls

Iterations on pattern B (click to zoom at cursor, drag to pan). Adds variable zoom levels and preview / minimap options. Same DeGuardia screenshot throughout.

B0. Baseline

Your pick

How: Click zooms to 2.25× at cursor; drag pans; click resets. No extra chrome — control reference.

B1. Zoom steps (− / +)

How: Same click/pan, plus toolbar buttons for 1× · 1.5× · 2× · 3× and − / +. Click still zooms toward the pointer if you’re at 1×.

B2. Continuous slider

How: Slider from 1×–4×; zoom keeps the current center (or last click focus). Drag to pan while zoomed; Fit resets.

B3. Corner minimap preview

How: When zoomed, a small overview appears in the corner with a viewport rectangle. Drag the minimap (or click it) to jump; main view still click-zooms and pans.

B4. Side preview + zoom chips

How: Persistent side overview (always visible) + 1× / 2× / 3× chips. Click overview to pan the main view; click main image to zoom toward cursor.

B5. Kitchen sink

Full

How: Slider + −/+ + Fit + corner minimap. Most control; densest chrome — useful to see where “too much” starts.
