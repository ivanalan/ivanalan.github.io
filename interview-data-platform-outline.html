8. What I’d do differently

Involve the customer earlier

Because this shipped for a specific customer, I should have involved them earlier. I had a Technical Account Manager close to them, but I treated the customer as a consumer rather than a stakeholder — partly to avoid breaking norms around how we engaged customers.

Instead of looping them in only at major milestones, I would have brought them into the initial scoping and requirements.

Talking points

  • One honest reverse — don’t stack self-critique.
  • If they ask “advocate for the user”: TAM existed; engagement norms made you too cautious.
