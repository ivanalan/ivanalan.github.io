## Context

On-call medical rotations in Mexico follow repeating patterns such as A-C and
A-D. Keeping those cycles in a calendar is easy to get wrong, and most tools
assume a network connection and an account.

## What it does

Guardias is a personal, offline-first tracker for those rotations. Schedule data
stays on the device. There is no sign-in and no Guardias backend in v1.
