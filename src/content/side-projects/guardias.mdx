## “When are you free?”

Every 4 days, my partner has a 24-hour overnight shift at the hospital. It is crucial to rest the night before a shift, the shift itself is physically and emotionally tiring, and the day after is dedicated to recovery. In short, overnight shifts are a key event in her schedule and daily life.

Yet, this 4-day cycle is a pattern that is not easily trackable on a 7-day weekly routine. Shifts always fall on different days of the week. So, everyone always asks the same question: “when are you free?”

## The job was never just tracking

Google Calendar and other medical tracker apps all solve the narrow version of this issue: repeat this event every four days. The needs I found go beyond that:

- Counting down the shifts and days left in a brutal rotation for motivation
- Anticipating when the one free weekend a month falls in order to plan a restful break.
- Sharing a calendar with others so they don’t have to keep asking
- Overlaying a friend’s shift schedule against their own

## Building > planning (sometimes)

When starting this project, I planned the information architecture (on paper!), documented requirements, edge cases, and researched different libraries. While I found the IA to be foundational (and did not change from my original sketches), other pre-work like my library choice changed multiple times (React Native Paper → Nativewind).

I learned that what’s important is to plan the basics, but build as quick as possible to iterate with feedback from real users. Decisions that were once rigid, like technical architecture, are now cheap to change with AI, so it's easier than ever to get a prototype in front of users instead of agonizing over those choices first.
