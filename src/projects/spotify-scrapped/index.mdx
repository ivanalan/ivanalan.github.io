## Outcome

1. Shipped [myscrapped.com](https://myscrapped.com): a Spotify anti-Wrapped for songs and artists that got ghosted.
2. Created an automated email system to notify users when they were off the waitlist.
3. Hacked around Spotify’s API by storing user data on a secure database, scaling a limited API restriction.

## The problem: My liked songs are full of junk

When I listen to music, I put on my liked songs and hit shuffle. More often than not, I’m skipping songs that I don’t listen to anymore, songs that are outdated, or songs that I outgrew.

I wanted a way to prune these songs.

## Design explorations: what qualifies as a song I should remove?

How do you quantify a “bad” song? I began by analyzing songs I always skip. However, I also ended up exploring different themes like total play times, artists with only one song saved, and all time favorites I haven’t listened to.

And thus, Spotify Scrapped was born. The idea was that instead of showing you all your top themes, a user could log in and see the opposite: all of their worst themes.

## Technical limitation: Spotify’s API

Spotify limits API usage to 5 users, who must be registered on your API. To scale this to the public, I came up with a waitlist. Users enter their email, it gets stored on a database, rotated with the last seat, and an email is sent giving them a link to sign into the experience.

The best part is that once the user signs in once, they can always see their story as they are sent a new email with a link to their story using their previous data to populate — without needing an API seat.

## Try it out!

The live site is at [myscrapped.com](https://myscrapped.com). Enter your email on the waitlist and expect an email invite!
